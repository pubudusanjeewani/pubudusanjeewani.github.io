Hello! I'm Pubudu Sanjeewani

Associate Lecturer · RMIT University

I am an Associate Lecturer in Artificial Intelligence at RMIT University, Melbourne. My research interests include deep learning, computer vision, explainable AI, generative AI, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. I hold a PhD in Artificial Intelligence and Image Processing from Central Queensland University, and I have previously worked as a research fellow, machine learning engineer and software engineer.

My current research focuses on the medical and health domain, where I develop methods for analysing multimodal medical data, including images (histopathology, CT, MRI), signals (ECG) and text, with a focus on explainable AI, generative AI, and large language and vision-language models. Earlier in my career, I applied these methods to road safety, detecting safety attributes from roadside video, and to workplace safety.

Research Projects

Funded Project · 2024

A novel automatic neural network feature extractor with explanations and non-iterative learning

Funded by Australian Research Council Discovery Project Grant, the project focused on developing an automatic neural network-based feature extraction framework with fast, non-iterative learning and explainable features. It investigated the feature extraction capabilities of convolutional and traditional neural networks, addressing challenges such as model complexity, training efficiency, and interpretability. The proposed approach aimed to support accurate and explainable feature extraction from both image and non-image data across diverse real-world applications.

With Griffith University, Australia. Supervisors: Prof. Abdul Sattar and Prof. Brijesh Verma.

Project Website
Industry Project · 2023

Secure multi-platform for identifying and protecting world-heritage Aboriginal UNESCO rock art sites in Far North Queensland

Developed an AI-powered digital platform using drone imagery, machine learning, and mapping technologies to identify, document, and preserve thousands of historically significant Aboriginal rock art sites in Far North Queensland. The project involved labelling and analysing drone footage to support the creation of a high-resolution digital archive of cultural heritage sites.

With KJR, Australia, and partners (Western Yalanji Aboriginal Corporation, Athena AI, Emesent, FlyFreely, MaxusAI, World of Drones Education Pty Ltd and Griffith University), funded by Advance Queensland, Queensland Government, and Trusted Autonomous Systems (TAS). Supervisors: Dr Kelvin Ross and Dr Mark Pedersen.

Project Website
Industry Project · 2022 – 2023

Automatic hard hat and safety vest detection, slip/trip/fall detection, and hooning (reckless driving) detection AI projects

Developed AI-powered video analytics solutions for real-time safety monitoring using CCTV cameras. The projects focused on detecting PPE non-compliance (hard hats and safety vests), identifying slips, trips and falls, and detecting hooning (reckless driving) through deep learning-based object detection and tracking, enabling automated alerts and incident monitoring.

With Smart AI Connect, MaxusAI (Australia) and Veracity AI (Sri Lanka). Supervisors: Christopher Lane, Glenn Neuber, John Fitzgerald and Stan Potums.

PhD Project · 2019 – 2022

A deep learning technique for the analysis of road safety attributes

Funded by the Australian Research Council (ARC) Linkage Projects, this project developed a deep learning-based automated system to analyse road videos, detect and classify road objects, and assess road safety conditions, aiming to improve road infrastructure assessment and reduce road fatalities.

Industry partners: Department of Transport and Main Roads (DTMR), Queensland, and the Australian Road Research Board (ARRB). Supervisors: Prof. Brijesh Verma and Dr Mary Tom.
